Whyzr for grown-ups

The journal records thinking, not surveillance.

Writing up the session that just finished. This takes up to a minute. The new entry will appear here on its own. 

    


    

      

        These are the rules your child's tutor must follow. Edit them and every
        change is saved as a git commit in your child's own copy. Nothing
        here can reach the public Whyzr repository.
        

      

      

        
        

          
          
          
        

        

          Two rules do the heavy lifting: never give the direct answer and
          one question per turn. Change them and Whyzr stops being Whyzr,
          though you can always restore the originals.
        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          Whyzr has five ways of asking a question. It keeps score of which ones
          get your child to the answer, and tries the best one next time.
          This is kept separately for every child, so it is about them and
          nobody else.
        

        

      

      

        
Why each score moved

        

          Graded by a second, separate AI that never sees the conversation, only
          the question and your child's final answer. Their answers are not
          recorded here.
