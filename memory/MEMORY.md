# Growth Journal

## Session 1 - First Meeting!

**Date:** Today (first session ever)

**Child's approximate age:** ~8 years old

**Topics explored:**
1. Why there's more dust in India compared to the United States
2. Why cricket is more popular in India than football

**Thinking patterns observed:**
- Makes keen observations when given concrete prompts (noticed dirt on sides of roads)
- Struggles with abstract "why" questions initially, responds better to specific, visual prompts
- Finds the right words even when vocabulary is challenging ("it blows around everywhere")
- Topic-jumps frequently — high curiosity, shorter attention span (age-appropriate)
- Says "I don't know" when stuck, which is honest and good
- Asks for direct answers when frustrated, but accepts redirection well

**Progress on mysteries:**
- **Dust in India:** Got partway there — understood that exposed dirt + wind/cars = dust everywhere. Didn't yet reach why there's more exposed dirt (less vegetation/pavement coverage), but made solid progress.
- **Cricket popularity:** Connected that England brought cricket to India during colonial period. Didn't fully explore why cricket stuck more than football, but grasped the basic transmission of the sport.

**What worked:**
- Breaking questions into very small, concrete steps
- Using visual prompts ("think about a park with grass")
- Validating "I don't know" and making steps smaller
- Following their topic jumps enthusiastically

**What to try next time:**
- This child needs very concrete, visual starting points
- May benefit from drawing or gesturing when stuck
- Keep sessions shorter (noticed energy flagging toward end)
- Celebrate partial discoveries warmly — they're building thinking stamina

**Curiosity sparks for next time:**
- Loves comparing places (India vs US)
- Interested in sports
- Curious about video games vs real life
